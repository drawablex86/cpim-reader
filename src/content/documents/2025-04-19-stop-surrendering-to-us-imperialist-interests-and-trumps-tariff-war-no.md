Howard Lutnick, the US Commerce Secretary, asserted that India should open its agricultural market and agriculture cannot be ‘off the table’ in the negotiation of the Bilateral Trade Agreement. It is reported that India has already signalled willingness to have a zero-duty framework on imports of specific quantities of lentils and peas in line with the agreement with Australia for import of fixed quantities of lentils at zero duty. In the context of huge subsidies to American farmers, it is very clear that any Bilateral Trade Agreement will be extremely unequal. Big American Commodity Cartels are raising the bogey of high domestic subsidies in India and trade barriers. The objective of the Trump threats is to undermine even the meagre protection that has been accorded to the agricultural sector with a large number of peasants and agricultural workers who are dependent on this sector for subsistence. The total population of the cultivators in India is higher than the total population of the US. The average tariff on agricultural products at 39% is nearly one third of the permissible bound rate under WTO. The recent tie-up of Airtel and Jio with Elon Musk’s company Starlink at the behest of the government even as the US President Trump continues to humiliate India is the latest such instance. Apart from agriculture, the interests of the MSMEs across sectors like generic pharmaceuticals to auto parts and millions of workers across these sectors are also going to be adversely hit.
