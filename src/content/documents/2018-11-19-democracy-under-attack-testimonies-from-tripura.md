# **Introduction** 

Since March 3, 2018, as the announcement of results for the Assembly elections in Tripura confirmed that the CPI(M)-led Left Front is being unseated from the state government by the BJPIPFT opportunistic alliance, a wave of widespread fascistic attacks were unleashed against the CPI(M) cadres and offices by the RSSBJP.  These attacks continue even today. 

These attacks have many dimensions.  At one level, there is an outright brutal murder of CPI(M) workers.  At another level, CPI(M) offices have been burnt or forcefully captured.  At yet another level, the homes of CPI(M) workers have been attacked, burnt and looted. 

Post the formation of the BJP-IPFT government, physical attacks on CPI(M) workers and the constant vigilance on their activities continue with the patronage of the State administration and the law and order machinery to enforce that CPI(M) workers desist from participation in any political activity.  This has resulted in an unprecedented situation of terror and violence in the state.  As a consequence, in the recent by-elections to local bodies, 96 per cent of the seats were won uncontested by the BJP as the opposition candidates were violently prevented from filing their nomination. 

Yet another level, there are attacks on CPI(M) workers and families aimed to eliminate their livelihood and to economically incapacitate them.

### **_Rakesh Dhar, killed on April 12. His body was recovered the following day._** 

The attack on democracy in Tripura has many dimensions. CPI(M) workers have been killed, Party offices burnt or forcibly captured, homes of Party workers attacked, burnt and looted. This started with the wave of violence on March 3, 2018, after the election results were announced. There are physical attacks on Party workers, the constant monitoring by BJP-RSS men of their activities, and further attacks if they participate in political activity. There are also attempts to destroy the livelihood of CPI(M) workers and supporters, to cripple them financially. 

These attacks take place in front of children, who are totally terrorised. Where families have had to shift, the education of children suffers. Some children have had to drop out of school or have been unable to appear for examinations. 

In many instances, the BJP men leading the attacks were earlier with the Congress. Some took part in the anti-communist violence under the Congress-TUJS regime in the late 1980s. 

The police and administration back the BJP-RSS miscreants. When CPI(M) workers are attacked, cases are filed against them, while the attackers get police protection! In other cases, where the FIR is filed, the police does not act and permits the perpetrators to bully, intimidate, threaten and once again attack the victims to force them to withdraw the FIRs. 

These interviews and experiences in this bookletare illustrative of a much wider use of anti-democratic, authoritarian and fascistic methods against Left supporters.
