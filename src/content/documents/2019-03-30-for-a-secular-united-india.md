# **Citizens for a Secular United India** 

Dear Citizens, 

The India that we love is under severe attack by those in power who have sought to divide her citizens in the name of religion. Those who hold the reins of power have never accepted the concept of a secular India with equal rights for all its citizens. They have always advocated the Hindu Rashtra as their goal where citizenship is decided on the basis of religion. The obnoxious Citizenship Amendment Bill to change the basis of citizenship to one’s religious denomination was the first step towards changing the constitutional framework. It was foiled by the people of the north-east supported by the entire opposition. But the danger is very great. 

At the same time India is faced with the threat of terrorist attacks which have taken innocent lives and which threaten India’s security.In Kashmir in particular the incidents of terror attacks have seen a rise of 176 per cent in the last five years of the Modi regime. The cross border support for these terror attacks continues, the most recent one being the horrific attack in Pulwama taking the lives of 40 CRPF personnel. The entire country is unified against these terror attacks. 

The CPI(M) has been unequivocal in its fight against terror. In Kashmir, the CPIM) cadre has waged an extremely courageous struggle for upholding constitutional values for democracy and against separatist forces, at great risk to their own lives. 

The CPI(M) has been in the forefront to uphold the values of secularism. We can proudly state that while some may have compromised with  the shift to soft Hindutva politics, the CPI(M) has steadfastly upheld the basic principle to keep religion separate from politics. 

The CPI(M) has raised its voice for a comprehensive legislation against communal violence including against hate speech. It has also demanded a law against lynchings, a phenomenon that has increased exponentially during the BJP regime.

This fight against communalism is an intrinsic part of the struggle for a better and stronger india. Secular values must be upheld to have an effective united fight against terror of all hues. But the Modi Government has weakened this unity by its communal approach and its attacks on the minorities. 

# **Attacks Against Minorities** 

The BJP-RSS and the Sangh Parivar organisations have created new records in communal speech, practice and policy.Among the various “issues” that have been manufactured by the Sangh parivar to spread this poison against Muslims and Christians are “love jihad”, forcible conversion, beef, population growth, allegiance to Pakistan or international jihadists, etc. 

These five years have seen a series of violent attacks and incidents against minority communities fuelled by incendiary statements from leaders, shielding of criminals from legal action, and a poisonous campaign of lies and hatred carried out amongst the people. Hindu festivals have been turned into armed celebrations that viciously target minority communities, whilereligious observances by minorities have been proscribed or attacked. Hindu fanatic elements have used ‘cow protection’ as a pretext to attack Muslims and Dalits.The issue of love jihad has been used to make the most provocative speeches in the name of protecting “our women.” All elections are vitiated by communal propaganda both overtly in public campaigning and covertly through hate-filled social media messaging. 

In many cases local police was reported to be complicit in the violence. In many states under BJP rule, especially UP, Muslim youth have been targeted for arrests and even “encounters” by trigger-happy police. Such is the impunity cultivated among mobs that even police officers were not spared in some cases.  As a counterpoint, cases against Hindu terrorists involved in Samjhauta Express, Mecca Masjid, Malegaon and other bomb blasts, as also against some of the key instigators of the Gujarat anti-Muslim pogrom of 2002, have been allowed to peter out due to lack of diligent prosecution. This has sent a clear message to violent Hindu extremists that the govt. is protecting them.

# **Incidents of Violence** 

The result of this barrage of Hindu fanatic and supremacist propaganda and violence is that the country’s social fabric and harmony is today under severe strain. 

- **Communal Violence** : Incidents of communal violence have increased by 28% between 2014 and 2017 (the last year for which official data is available). In these four years, there have been nearly 3000 communal incidents which have taken the toll of about 400 lives and left nearly 9000 people injured. 

Officially registered cases of inciting communal violence have gone up by 41% in this period from 366 in 2014 to 475 in 2017. 

In the first four months of 2018, 13 districts of Bihar, 3 of Rajasthan and 3 of W.Bengal were rocked by communal violence directly instigated by armed processions organised and led by Sangh Parivar associates or BJP leaders on the occasion of Hindu festival of Ram Navami. 

- **Lynching** : Since the ascent of Modi, there have been 122 cases of mob attacks and lynching spread across 19 states. These horrific attacks caused deaths of 46 people and left 294 injured, including 148 seriously injured. Almost all of these attacks were led or egged on by Sangh Parivar associated persons. 

Over two thirds of the victims were Muslims and the remaining mostly Dalits. These incidents were sparked off by wild and manufactured rumours of beef eating, cow slaughter, etc. In some cases, like Junaid’s lynching in Faridabad, a minor incident in a train turned into lynching by a hate filled mob. In most cases, the killers are either free or the trials are stuck in court. BJP and Sangh parivar leaders have openly sided with the criminals. 

- **Rape as Communal Weapon** : In a barbaric incident in January 2018, an eight year old Bakarwal girl was abducted, kept in a place of worship and repeatedly raped before being murdered in Kathua, Jammu. The reasons motivating the criminals included their desire to get rid of the Bakerwal Muslims who had settled near their village. RSS-BJP and its

- **Attacks on Christians** : Another minority community in the country, the Christians, too are facing growing violence from the Sangh Parivar. An estimated 1200 attacks have taken place on churches, clergy, carol singers, Christmas and Easter events and missionaries across the country in the past 5 years. Many such incidents are not reported to the police and go unrecorded because the victims are terrorised. 

- **Killings of Rationalists** : The killings of Kaburgi, Pansare, Dabholkar and Gauri Lankesh by Hindu fanatics shows another facet of the Sangh parivar’s diabolic intentions of targeting voices of reason and progressive thought. Investigations by the Karnataka police have clearly established that the Sanatan Sanstha, a Hindutva outfit with connections to the Sangh Parivar, had undertaken these killings, trained the assassins, accumulated arms and set up a hit list of many other progressive persons. 

The govt. on its part, has mostly kept silent on all of these issues and the violence that has been perpetrated, barring some hypocritical and anodyne statements. Behind the scenes, however, it is clear that perpetrators of violence are shielded, while BJP leaders become hyper active in case a Muslim person is involved in a crime. 

The efforts to communalise society have created mutual friction and tensions in several parts of the country, even in places where there was minimal history of such strife. In all these cases, the handiwork of RSS affiliated groups, that have mushroomed all over, can be seen. This dangerous strategy is also pushing sections of the minority communities into the arms of minority fundamentalists thus further alienating them.This is a very dangerous development as one form of communalism strengthens the other leading to the most serious repercussions.

A series of religious congregations were convened to issue strident calls for building the Ram Temple at the disputed site in Ayodhya. The Yogi govt. threw its full weight behind this and the RSS leadership actively campaigned for it. They repeatedly asserted that faith is bigger than the Courts. However, since common people did not show much enthusiasm for this attempt, and the Supreme Court did not get pushed into a quick hearing to the pending dispute, the RSS and its

# **Citizenship Bill** 

Another blatant display of the Modi govt.’s communal agenda took place when they tried to pass the Citizenship Amendment Bill which allowed all refugees from neighbouring countries barring Muslims to become Indian citizens. This stirred up a hornets nest in the north eastern states with sustained protests because it violated the Assam Accord of 1985, and threatened to tear apart the fragile relations between various ethnic groups in the region.
