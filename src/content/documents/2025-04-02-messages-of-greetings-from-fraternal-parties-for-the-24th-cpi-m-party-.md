# **COMMUNIST PARTY OF CHINA (CPC)** 

18 March 2025, Beijing 

# Dear Comrades, 

Delighted to learn that the Communist Party of India (Marxist) [CPI(M)] will convene its 24<sup>th</sup> Party Congress, the International Department of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China (CPC) wishes to extend our warmest congratulations to you and wishes the Congress a complete success. 

As an important left force in India with a century-long history, the CPI(M) has long been committed to advancing economic and social progress of India.  We  believe that the Congress will serve as an opportunity for the CPI(M) to achieve new development and made contributions to promoting regional prosperity and progress. 

The international landscape and world order are now in a new round of transformation.  An improving China-India  relationship serves the common interests of both countries and the region. The CPC and the CPI(M) have long maintained friendly exchanges, which has played a significant role for facilitating mutual understanding and practical cooperation between our two sides. Under the new circumstances, the CPC stands ready to strengthen exchanges and strategic communication as well as experience sharing of party and state governance with the CPI(M) and other Indian political parties, so as to promote  continued progress of China-India relations as well as peace and stability of the region. 

# **International Department Central Committee Communist Party of China**

On behalf of the Central Committee and the entire membership of the South African Communist Party (SACP), we extend our warmest revolutionary greetings and unwavering solidarity to the leadership and delegates of the Communist Party of India (Marxist) gathered for your XXIV Congress. 

We are honoured to share a message of support on this important occasion. As your friend and ally we continue to stand with you as we confront fascism, capitalism and imperialism. We also stand with you as we celebrate the creation of a more internationalist world on the path of communism. 

We wish all delegates the very best as you undertake critical discussions to analyse the current political situation, adopt your political and organisational resolutions, and elect new leadership to carry forward the historic mission of your Party. India currently finds itself in a difficult place, or, should we say, the poor, marginalised and working class who form the majority of Indians citizens, find themselves under a regime that seems to put the interests of the elite and of a chosen few above those of the collective nation. 

These conditions under which you find yourselves — an increasingly repressive and chauvinistic ruling party in India — are extremely challenging. Yet, the courage, discipline, and clarity of purpose with which the CPI(M) confronts these conditions is a testament to the resilience and rootedness of your Party among India’s workers, peasants, and oppressed peoples. 

Thus, the SACP recognises the CPI(M) as a steadfast and principled force in the international communist movement. Indeed, your consistent struggle against neoliberalism, religious sectarianism, right-wing authoritarianism, and imperialist domination has been an inspiration to us and to many around the world. 

As South African communists, we are painfully familiar with the brutal consequences of racial and sectarian politics, and we fully understand the danger posed by forces that seek to divide the working class along lines of religion, caste, ethnicity, or language. We salute the CPI(M) for its unyielding commitment to class unity, social justice, and democratic rights in the face of such adversity. We further note with

appreciation your Party’s important work in mass movements, including the mass women’s movement and trade unions, and your efforts to build the broadest possible alliances in defence of democracy and the working class. These efforts are translated into your presence both on the ground but also within the corridors of political and state power. 

We face common challenges across the Global South — deepening inequality, capitalist underdevelopment, ecological destruction, and the imperialist agenda of global monopoly capital. In this context, the role of communist and workers’ parties in building solidarity, developing alternatives, and leading the masses in the struggle for socialism is more vital than ever. The SACP is committed to deepening our bilateral relations with the CPI(M), learning from your rich experience, and contributing to a renewed and united international communist movement. We therefore look forward to your deliberations from this upcoming congress. 

As you deliberate in Madurai, please know that your South African comrades are following your work with admiration and solidarity. May your XXIV Congress be a great success, and may it strengthen the CPI(M) for the battles and breakthroughs ahead. 

Long live the Communist Party of India (Marxist)! Long live international working-class solidarity! Forward to socialism! 

**South African Communist Party (SACP) 15th Congress Central Committee March 2025**

# **COMMUNIST PARTY OF TURKEY (TKP)** 

March 6, 2025 

# Dear comrades, 

We would like to salute the XXIV Congress of the Communist Party of India (Marxist). During its long and rich history, CPI(M) has been the vanguard of the Indian working class for many decades. As a party with a strong base among the Indian people, it has accumulated important experiences. The party has been carrying the torch of socialism in one of the most important countries not only of Asia but also of the whole world. And the struggle is going on with a strong social support. 

The fact that India is governed by a reactionary, religious sectarian party makes the current situation even more arduous. The Modi government has adopted authoritarian methods, similar to those in an increasing number of countries. Another issue is that this government is cooperating closely with several imperialist countries and trying to benefit from the fierce competition between them. 

These facts make the struggle for socialism even more crucial. The task of CPI(M) as the vanguard in this struggle is of utmost importance. In a country with a vast working class and a rich history of social struggle, strengthening of CPI(M) will be a source of inspiration for all communist forces in the world. 

With the new US administration and rapidly evolving developments in the international arena, we may witness a new era, in which the international correlation of forces can change drastically. Under these circumstances, international solidarity and international cooperation between communist parties become even more crucial. 

CPI(M) and the Communist Party of Turkey have always had close relations in the past. We are confident that these relations and cooperation between our parties will improve in the near future, as the international communist movement has to increase its influence in world politics. We are ready to do our part in this regard. 

With our comradely greetings, 

**Communist Party of Turkey Central Committee**

# **COMMUNIST PARTY OF USA (CPUSA)** 

# Dear comrades, 

The Communist Party USA extends its warmest fraternal greetings to the 24th Congress of the Communist Party of India (Marxist) and expresses its resolute solidarity with the struggles of the working and oppressed peoples of India. We further congratulate the Party on the recent conclusion of its 24th State Congress in Kerala. 

More than ever, the convergence of corporate and neo-fascist organizations in India and the United States calls for deepened fraternal solidarity and collaboration between our Parties. In its course of development, the American extreme right has strongly emphasized its relationship with the BJP as a pillar of its legitimacy. A program of mutual interference, termed “MEGA” by PM Modi as a nod to the MAGA movement, has facilitated far-right advances in both India and the United States at the behest of the most chauvinistic elements of monopoly capital. This program has developed through years of targeted VHP organizing among the Indian diaspora, and saw significant elaboration of its economic and geopolitical terms during the February 13th meeting between Trump and Modi. 

The CPUSA firmly defends the CPI(M) in the face of ever-expanding codependence of MAGA and Hindutva forces. As the Trump administration relies on the BJP as a keystone of its imperialist ambitions and the Modi government and Trump regime take their cues from each other, our Parties stand shoulder to shoulder in opposition. Comrades, we look to your heroic advances in revolutionary strategy and workingclass governance not only as inspiration, but as a bulwark in the struggle against reaction. 

We wish a productive and invigorating 24th congress to the delegates and members of the CPI(M), and salute your contributions to the shared struggles ahead. 

Long live working-class internationalism! 

# **International Department, Communist Party USA**

# **COMMUNIST PARTY OF VENEZUELA (PCV)** 

# Dear Comrades 

Communist Party of India (Marxist) 

The Communist Party of Venezuela sends warm Marxist-Leninist greetings to all the militants, friends and sympathizers of the glorious Communist Party of India (Marxist) on the occasion of its XXIV Congress, to be held April 2-6 in Madurai, Tamilnadu. 

Its Congress is taking place at a time of profound crisis of the world imperialist system, which in the framework of its fierce competition for the control of resources, markets and trade routes, increases wars, invasions, policies of interference, and encourages the advance and strengthening of ultra-reactionary political expressions such as the BJP. 

Like the people of India, the Venezuelan people are also struggling against an authoritarian government strongly influenced by conservative religious sectors, which today is attacking their democratic rights and social conquests. Contradictorily, this type of governments, deeply reactionary due to their openly capitalist and anti-popular character, present themselves as “anti-imperialist” alternatives due to their supposed contradictions with the powers of the traditional imperialist pole. 

From the Communist Party of Venezuela (PCV), we reiterate our solidarity with the working people of India and with the Communist Party of India (Marxist), in their struggle against the reactionary government of Modi and the BJP, and for the conquest of more rights and liberties for the working class. 

It is propitious the occasion to express our gratitude for the solidarity of the CPI(M) with our organization and the Venezuelan working class, in the face of the governmental onslaught against the democratic right to exist of the PCV and the restrictions to the trade union freedoms in our country. 

From the PCV, we wish much success in its XXIV Congress, to the new Central Committee that will be elected, and we are sure that from this congressional process the PCI (M) will come out much stronger and with a greater influence in the class struggle in India. 

Long live the XXIV Congress of the CPI(M)! Long live proletarian internationalism! 

**Central Committee of the Communist Party of Venezuela**
