# **COMMUNIST PARTY OF INDIA (MARXIST) 21**<sup>**st**</sup> **Congress** 

**Samar Mukherjee Nagar Visakhapatnam, April 14-19, 2015** 

# **Draft Resolution** 

# **On the Centenary Year of the Great October Socialist Revolution** 

The 21st Congress of the Communist Party of India (Marxist) notes that the year November 7, 2016 to November 7, 2017 marks the 100th anniversary year of one of the greatest events in the history of humankind, the Great October Socialist Revolution. 

The October Revolution established the first state and society committed to the end of the exploitation of human being by human being and the oppression of one nation by another. The October Revolution laid the basis for the major socio-political achievements of the working class and other toiling people in the twentieth century. The October Revolution and the establishment of the USSR inspired the establishment of Communist Parties in different parts of the world, inspired the movements for freedom and independence from the colonial powers, inspired the movements for universal employment, social security, and health care, and was the decisive factor in the destruction of Nazism, fascism, and Japanese militarism in World War II. It established women’s equality in all spheres. 

The 21st Congress of the CPI(M) calls for celebrations to commemorate the centenary year in all-round way. It calls on the new Central Committee to organise events to commemorate the October Revolution in the political, ideological, and cultural spheres, and to conduct a campaign to propagate socialism.
