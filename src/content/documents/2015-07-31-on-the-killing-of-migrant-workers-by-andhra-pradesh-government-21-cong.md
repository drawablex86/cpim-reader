## **COMMUNIST PARTY OF INDIA (MARXIST) 21**<sup>**st**</sup> **Congress Samar Mukherjee Nagar Visakhapatnam, April 14-19, 2015** 

# **<mark>Resolution</mark>** 

# **<mark>On the Killing Of Migrant Workers by Andhra Pradesh Government</mark>** 

<mark>The 21</mark><sup>st</sup> <mark>Congress of Communist Party of India (Marxist) strongly condemns the cold-blooded killing of 20 migrant workers, including 13 tribal people, from Tamil Nadu by the Andhra Pradesh police in the Seshachalam forest in Andhra Pradesh. The State Government of Andhra Pradesh, which has failed to check smuggling and illegal trade in red sanders in the Seshachalam forest by taking firm action against smugglers and illegal traders, most of whom have close links with top political leaders of the State, has resorted to brutal killing of the workers.</mark> 

<mark>The 21</mark><sup>st</sup> <mark>Congress of the CPI (M) demands that a CBI enquiry be instituted into the incident and that the Andhra Pradesh Government provide full compensation to the families of those killed. The police who have resorted to these inhuman killings should be stringently punished.</mark>
